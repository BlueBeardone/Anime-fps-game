# Anime FPS Game — Bolt-Action Rifle, Held Viewmodel and Muzzle Tracers

Date: 2026-11-18
Status: awaiting approval

## Intent

Make the first-person view show the K98 rifle held in front of the camera, make the Rifle slot behave like a
bolt-action rifle (fire → 1 s bolt cycle → fire, with a normal reload after 5 rounds), and make tracers and
the muzzle flash come out of the gun instead of the camera.

Why now: the game plays well, but the owner sees an empty screen because the viewmodel is rotated sideways,
and the Rifle currently behaves like a 30-round full-auto. This is the first polish pass on the weapons.

## What already exists (and why the gun is invisible)

- A per-slot viewmodel system already spawns `K98_Viewmodel.prefab` under the camera `ViewModel` anchor,
  with procedural recoil kick and a reload dip (`PlayerWeapons`).
- `K98_prefab.prefab` holds the K98 with **no yaw**; its child transform already points the barrel forward.
  Every slot instead configures `viewmodelLocalEuler = (0, 90, 0)`, which turns the rifle sideways and off
  the right edge of the frame.
- `MaterialLocation.External is obsolete` warnings come from the legacy FBX `.meta` files via Unity's
  QuickSearch indexer. The K98 mesh still imports, and its prefab references real URP materials. These
  warnings are **not** the cause and are out of scope.
- The `ViewModel` anchor is empty in the prefab/scene view because the gun spawns at runtime; this is normal.
- Tracers and the muzzle flash currently start at the camera origin.

## Goals

- Rifle viewmodel is visible, centred on the right hand side of the screen, barrel pointing forward.
- Rifle slot is a 5-round bolt action: one shot, 1.0 s bolt cycle, then the existing 2.2 s reload when the
  magazine is empty (or on R).
- Tracers and muzzle flash originate at the rifle muzzle for the owner and for other players.
- The camera ray stays the source of truth for aim and server validation, so the crosshair remains honest.
- Other weapon slots (Handgun, SMG, Shotgun) are untouched.
- Existing PlayMode tests stay green; new tests cover the bolt cycle.

## Non-goals

- No first-person arms/hands rig. The viewmodel stays a floating gun for this pass.
- No authored bolt/reload animation, no new art, no audio.
- No changes to damage falloff, headshots, ADS, or the other three guns.
- No reimport/repair of the legacy FBX material settings.

## Anti-goals

- Moving the raycast origin to the muzzle (would make the crosshair lie and could clip walls).
- Per-weapon duplicate firing code.
- Requiring `Anime FPS > Run All` for the fix to work.

## Constraints

- Unity 6000.6.9f1, URP, Netcode for GameObjects 2.14.0.
- `Rifle.CreateDefaultStats()` and `Rifle.asset` both drive slot 0; `Run All` never overwrites existing assets,
  so the serialized asset must be updated too.
- `Player.prefab` is regenerated by `ProjectSetup`, so any slot transform change must be made in both the
  prefab and `ProjectSetup`.
- `PlayerWeaponTests` asserts slot 0's archetype is `GunArchetype.Rifle`, so the archetype must not change.
- Gun logic stays plain C# and testable without a scene.

## Approaches considered

1. **Repair the existing rig (chosen).** Correct the Rifle slot orientation, derive a muzzle point from the
   viewmodel, and add a `BoltAction` fire mode with a bolt-cycle stat.
   Cheap, reuses working code, keeps other guns untouched, needs one short tuning pass after playing.
2. **Runtime auto-fit every gun.** Derive orientation/muzzle from renderer bounds for all slots.
   Generic, but fragile on multi-part guns and removes artist control. Rejected.
3. **Full first-person arms rig.** Clone the soldier's arms onto the camera with an Animator.
   Most immersive, but needs a separate viewmodel camera/layer and risks the working game. Deferred.

## Design

### 1. Viewmodel orientation and placement

- Rifle slot `viewmodelLocalEuler` becomes `(0, 0, 0)` in both `ProjectSetup.BuildPlayerPrefab` and the
  serialized `Player.prefab`. This matches how the soldier prefab holds the K98 (its child transform already
  converts the mesh barrel to +Z).
- Keep the existing hip position `(0.24, -0.22, 0.48)` and scale `0.08`; these are placeholder values and may
  be tuned once in the Inspector after playing.
- No shared-prefab edit is needed for visibility.

### 2. Muzzle point and effect origin

- `PlayerWeapons` computes a local muzzle offset when a slot is applied:
  - prefer a child named `Muzzle` on the viewmodel, if one exists;
  - otherwise take the combined renderer bounds and pick the point farthest along the viewmodel's local +Z
    (the barrel axis after the orientation fix).
- The local offset is converted to world space every shot, so it follows recoil and reload motion.
- `ShotSpec` / the camera ray are unchanged: raycasts and server validation still use the camera origin.
- Local effects: `ShotEffects.PlayLocal(muzzleOrigin, rayOrigin, directions, hits)` starts tracers and the
  flash at the muzzle and ends them at the actual hit point.
- Remote effects: `FireServerRpc` and `FireEffectClientRpc` carry the shooter's muzzle world position; the
  server sanity-checks it is within 3 m of the shooter (same rule as the ray origin) and rebroadcasts it.
  Remote clients draw tracers from that muzzle to the server-provided hit points.
- The muzzle flash point light moves to the muzzle origin.

### 3. Bolt action

- Add `FireMode.BoltAction`.
- Add `GunStats.boltCycleSeconds` (default 0) and `GunStats.boltRecoilDegrees` (default zero).
- `Gun.TryFire`:
  - `BoltAction` requires `triggerPressed`, exactly like `SemiAuto`; holding the trigger does not auto-fire.
  - after a successful shot, the next shot is locked for `boltCycleSeconds` (falling back to the RPM interval
    when zero);
  - for `BoltAction`, recoil is the fixed `boltRecoilDegrees` every shot instead of the spray-pattern delta
    (a 1 s cycle always exceeds `sprayResetSeconds`, so the pattern would otherwise reset and give zero kick).
- Add `BoltCycleProgress01` to `IGun`/`Gun` so the viewmodel can dip/rotate slightly during the cycle
  (procedural placeholder for a bolt animation). Reload progress and HUD behaviour are unchanged.
- `Rifle.CreateDefaultStats()` and `Rifle.asset` become:
  - `fireMode = BoltAction`, `magazineSize = 5`, `reserveAmmo = 30`, `reloadSeconds = 2.2`,
    `damage = 55`, `boltCycleSeconds = 1.0`, `boltRecoilDegrees = (0.4, 2.2)`.
  - Range, spread and bloom values stay as they are.
- Auto-reload after the fifth round uses the existing empty-magazine rule and the 2.2 s timer.
- HUD needs no change: it already reads magazine/reserve and reload progress from the gun.

### 4. Tests

- Update `GunTests.TestStats()` to force `FullAuto` with `boltCycleSeconds = 0`, so the existing cadence,
  bloom and spray tests keep testing the generic loop.
- New tests:
  - bolt action fires once per press and ignores a held trigger;
  - cannot fire again inside the 1 s cycle, can fire after it;
  - firing the fifth round starts the normal reload;
  - bolt-action recoil is applied on every shot (including the first).
- `PlayerWeaponTests` must stay green (archetype remains `Rifle`).

## My calls (correctable)

- Keep `GunArchetype.Rifle` and drive the bolt action from `FireMode`, so the existing test and save data stay
  valid.
- Raise Rifle damage to 55 (two shots at 100 HP) so the 5-round bolt action is not worse than the SMG.
- Give the bolt action a fixed recoil kick instead of a spray pattern.
- Derive the muzzle from renderer bounds so no prefab surgery is required; a `Muzzle` child still overrides it.
- Keep the viewmodel gun-only; arms are a later pass.
- Leave the obsolete FBX material warnings alone.

## What is left to the builder

- The final hip position/scale after one playtest (the current numbers are close, not final).
- Whether the procedural bolt dip should be rotation, position, or both.
- Whether to add a dedicated `Muzzle` child later for exact per-weapon control.

## Risks

- The `Player.prefab` is an old-format prefab; the Euler change is a one-line serialized value and should be
  safe, but verify the prefab still opens.
- `Rifle.asset` is serialized YAML; new fields on `GunStats` deserialize with defaults, so existing assets do
  not break.
- The remote muzzle is cosmetic and client-reported; the server only sanity-checks distance.
